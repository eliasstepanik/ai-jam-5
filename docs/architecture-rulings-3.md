## 3. Known weaknesses (accepted)

Honest residual issues, accepted for the jam (SAE3 §1.6). None blocks implementation; each has a Day-4 measurement.

1. **No pre-Beacon exit.** O1 is rejected, so "deeper or out?" is "overspend vs back-fill", not "leave".
2. **Cluster dominance.** At mean, plain mid and beginner runs face no gate decision: the Beacon cluster (8 E detour for ≥ 62 E) always pays. Only overspend (greed, sodium, hit variance) and experts skipping the cluster meet the gate. Fix only via SAE3 §5.3 knobs 1–3.
3. **Back-fill into Z3 is worthless for the Beacon.** Cap 100 plus front-loading wipe it at the Z4 large; it only lifts the Z4 dip.
4. **Beginner Z4 dip 4–18 E;** at DMAX the beginner reaches the cluster at ≈ 0 E. Means only; log flame-outs.
5. **Post-cluster soft-lock by variance.** A devourer hit (35 E) in the last 20 tiles can leave < 40 E with all near-path resin used. Rule #14 does not cover it; only unexplored Z4 off-path resin remains.
6. **Escape share 19–25 %** of run time (SAE1 FLAG 2), not 12–18 %; escape rules are frozen.
7. **Generator thresholds unmeasured.** `CELL_FLOOR_MIN` (R-114), pocket counts (R-116) and the ≥ 50 / 62 attempt-0 rate are estimates until the first generator PR runs the cave check.

**Open numeric assumptions, calibrate on Day 4** (SAE3 §5, seeds 42, 7, 1234):

| Zone time, mid | 67 s (SAE2 §0, used everywhere) vs ≈ 77 s (SAE1 §5 with fight stops) | logged `t` at zone borders, run A | at 77 s mid cluster arrival ≈ 69.4 E; re-pin simulator asserts; knob 8 |

## 6. Player animation delivery update (2026-10-18)

**R-154 — User-approved player art contract.** Supersedes the older player-only facing, dimensions, walk count and separate-torch statements in the sprite catalog, style guide, render passes and asset pipeline. Player frames are 64×64 with pivot (32,32), eight separately authored directions per action, and separate torch/no-torch sets. Walk is 4 frames per direction per set; other action counts and fps are unchanged. The stick is integrated into torch-set frames; no `player_torch`. Flames and glow remain procedural. Player manifest ids are `player_body` (no torch) and `player_body-torch`, with action-direction animation keys such as `walk-ne`. The full player target is 384 frames and the full atlas catalog 616 frames across 45 ids. No claim of completed delivery is implied.

**R-155 — User-approved monster art contract (2026-10-18).** Supersedes older monster canvas sizes, right-facing-only art and 16-step runtime actor rotation in the sprite catalog, style guide, render passes and asset pipeline. Lurker, Stalker and Devourer body and separate eye frames are all 64×64, pivot (32,32). Every body and eye action has eight separately authored directions (`e,se,s,sw,w,nw,n,ne`), selected from facing using nearest 45° with clockwise halfway ties; use action-direction keys such as `lunge-ne`, `telegraph_a-sw` and `blink-n`. Never rotate or mirror actor sprite art. Body and eyes share facing; death holds its initial direction. Existing per-direction frame counts and fps stay unchanged. Default monster draw scales 0.5/0.75/1.5 preserve prior world footprints and collision radii 9/14/30. Monster target: 160 + 160 + 192 = 512 frames; full catalog: 1064 frames across the same 45 ids. R-154 remains the player contract; its historical 616-frame atlas total is superseded. The full untrimmed target exceeds the existing four-sheet ceiling; delivered packing must be measured and a full-delivery packing/budget decision remains open. This documents the target, not completed art or runtime integration.

Implementation defaults to preserve existing gameplay: draw player source at scale 0.5, keep collision radius 10 and 32-unit tiles; clockwise direction order `e,se,s,sw,w,nw,n,ne`, nearest-angle selection with clockwise half ties, no rotation/mirroring of authored player art. Torch-set per-frame source-pixel tip anchors drive procedural flame placement; fallback uses matching frame metadata. Allow up to four 1024² atlas sheets based on padded-area growth, while retaining byte budgets pending measurement. Monster dimensions and rotation remain unchanged.
